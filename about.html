The Institution

Greet Evening Degree College, Pachrukhi

Greet Evening Degree College is part of the GREET Group of Institutions at Pachrukhi, Rajdhanwar (Giridih, Jharkhand) — the same campus and the same team behind Greet Teacher Training College and GREET Nursing College, which have served the region's students for years.

The degree college offers three undergraduate programmes — B.A., B.Com. and B.Sc., each a four-year degree — carrying the group's focus on disciplined, affordable, career-oriented education into higher studies.

Affiliation

The college's programmes are affiliated to Vinoba Bhave University, Hazaribag — the state university whose degrees, examinations and results govern undergraduate study across the region.
