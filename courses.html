Courses Offered

Programmes of Study

Three undergraduate programmes — B.A., B.Com. and B.Sc. — each a four-year degree affiliated to Vinoba Bhave University, Hazaribag.

B.A. B.Com. B.Sc.
01

B.A. — Bachelor of Arts

4 years · Intermediate (10+2)

The humanities and social sciences degree. B.A. study builds reading, writing and reasoning across subjects like language, history, political science and sociology — the classic foundation for teaching, administration and further study.

Eligibility

  • Passed Intermediate (10+2)

Duration

  • 4 years

Where it leads

  • Teaching and education-related paths
  • M.A. and other postgraduate study
  • Government and competitive examinations
02

B.Com. — Bachelor of Commerce

4 years · Intermediate (10+2)

The commerce degree. B.Com. covers accounting, business studies and economics — the working knowledge behind shops, offices, banks and enterprises, and the base for professional finance courses.

Eligibility

  • Passed Intermediate (10+2)

Duration

  • 4 years

Where it leads

  • Accounting, banking and business roles
  • M.Com. and postgraduate study
  • Foundations for professional courses like CA / CS / CMA
03

B.Sc. — Bachelor of Science

4 years · Intermediate (10+2)

The science degree. B.Sc. study builds a foundation in the natural and physical sciences — the route into laboratory work, science teaching and postgraduate research.

Eligibility

  • Passed Intermediate (10+2)

Duration

  • 4 years

Where it leads

  • Laboratory and technical roles
  • Science teaching paths
  • M.Sc. and research-oriented higher study
